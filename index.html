One day to full marks

The course plan says that for speaking you must: introduce yourself, say what you like and dislike and how you feel, ask and answer simple questions, talk about hobbies, spell in French, and pronounce e, é, u, ou and liaison correctly. You're examined in a group of three, so expect to ask your partners questions as well as answer them. Tick blocks off as you go; ticks stay on this device.

In scope (sessions 1–10): introductions, s'appeler / être / avoir / aller / prendre / faire, age, spelling, nationalities, directions, places in town, negation, numbers 0–101, quel / quelle, colours, plurals, family, hobbies and sports, likes and dislikes, in the classroom. Not in scope: food, clothes and restaurant (those come after the oral).
On the day: speak slowly and finish your words. Short correct sentences beat long broken ones. If you don't understand, say « Pardon, vous pouvez répéter, s'il vous plaît ? » or « Plus lentement, s'il vous plaît. » Say « Bonjour madame / monsieur » when you walk in and « Merci, au revoir ! » when you leave.
